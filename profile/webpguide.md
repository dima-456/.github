# A guide to the .webp format

WebP is an image format that is widely supported on modern browsers. It maintains transparency, like .png files, and can support animation, like .gifs. WebP files are much smaller then .jps or .pngs, and are the file format we will be using to host images for our portofolio sites. 

## How to save images as a .webp in Photoshop

Open the image you want to convert to a .webp in Photoshop. In the menu bar, go to File > Save As Copy.

![saveascopy](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/74f0a7b1-9949-4fd8-a16e-6bf4e0917a54)

Rename your image, making sure to not use any special characters (including spaces). From the format dropdown, choose WebP.

![selectformat](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bd92aaa5-400b-4479-acdc-ffb3c9fdeb82)

Choose what quality you would like your image to be. I have been doing 63, which is the lowest "high-quality" option. For images that will be really small on your site, such as a logo, you can experiement with going smaller.

![webpoptions](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0e165f65-51cd-4137-8363-873c8baaaa12)
